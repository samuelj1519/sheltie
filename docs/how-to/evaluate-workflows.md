# Evaluate workflow effectiveness

English | [简体中文](evaluate-workflows.zh-CN.md)

Evaluate whether a method combined with Sheltie improves real tasks. Freeze adopted scope, comparison conditions, quality, and budget first; retain protocol/evidence in the change package without assuming benefit.

## Fix comparison subjects first

Use real unresolved tasks. Record users/goals/initial candidates/full methods/models/hosts/independent quality/budget/stops. Native and Sheltie groups use equivalent targets/method content/quality/authorization. Seriously configure native stages/review/delivery too.

Record method creation, first input, and later reuse separately. Actor usage history determines first use, not sample numbers. Executor changes, rehearsals, completing another group first, task leakage, and cross-group learning can affect comparisons; record them separately.

Combined trials reveal friction without isolating benefits of selection/revocation/export/preflight. For attribution, test one increment on new equivalent tasks with preregistered conditions; replaying solved tasks cannot establish before/after learning gains.

## Facts to retain

| Category | Minimum record |
| --- | --- |
| Identity/input | sample/run/actor/arm, actual usage history, model/host/binary, frozen task/initial candidate |
| Activity | Every participant's preparation/configuration/execution/checking/rework/takeover/delivery/acceptance and actual intervals |
| Waiting/cost | Wall time, tool/model waits, raw usage, actual payments/sources; unknown as null/unknown |
| Results | Actual candidate/patch/check output/final results/independent quality/out-of-flow acceptance/incompletion/stops |
| Interruption | Natural/injected, cause, actual reopening/actor change, resume inputs/repeated work |

Do not double-count overlapping activity for one person. Separate common preparation, per-group maintenance, per-run work, and experimental measurement/analysis. Attempt durations include waiting, without replacing human timing or model costs.

## Judge quality and cost together

An uninvolved reviewer checks actual candidates/deliveries against frozen quality. Neutral IDs may hide group cues without changing code/checks/necessary provenance. Record bias when blinding is impossible. Routine review, flow success, gate, and user acceptance remain separate.

Include costs of unaccepted/failed/blocked tasks, not only successful samples. Report raw samples, preparation/maintenance, per-run costs, quality, and gaps by group. Do not calculate savings with unknown costs. Thresholds are adopted criteria for this trial, without general product promises.

Stop affected runs for quality loss, exhausted budget, input drift, or missing environments. Preserve actual results without relaxing standards. Outcomes may narrow methods, improve instructions, defer mechanisms, or stop directions.
