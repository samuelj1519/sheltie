# Support

Start with the [documentation map](docs/en/README.md) and [troubleshooting guide](docs/en/how-to/troubleshoot.md). Verify whether you are using released v0.2.0 or the unreleased source candidate, and use its matching management root.

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/samuelj1519/sheltie/issues) with your version, platform, relevant command, expected and actual result, and a minimal Workbook where applicable. Remove private content and credentials. English is the default; Chinese reports are welcome.

Use [security reporting](SECURITY.md) for sensitive vulnerabilities. Support does not imply an availability or response-time commitment.
