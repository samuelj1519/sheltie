# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss concrete behavior, evidence, and tradeoffs; critique work without attacking the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable. Respect language differences and make technical discussions understandable to newcomers.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation. Apply these expectations consistently to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

To report sensitive conduct concerns, request a private contact through the repository's [issue tracker](https://github.com/samuelj1519/sheltie/issues) without publishing personal details. Public disagreement alone is not a conduct violation.
